# Persist recommendation history with limited evidence

JobLens stores Candidate-owned Recommendation History so people can revisit why a Job Listing was recommended, even though it does not retain the original Resume document, full Sanitized Resume, or deep Analysis Result. Each history entry includes the Job Listing identity and original link, the Candidate's selected Job Preferences, recommendation reasons, and paired verified Job Posting requirement and approved Resume excerpts. This convenience creates persistent sensitive data; an entry is created only when the Job Source permits retaining its posting citation and access control protects the paired evidence. Otherwise the recommendation remains available only in the current session, while a separate bookmark may still be saved if permitted. A history entry expires after 180 days and can be deleted individually at any time; account deletion removes all remaining entries. Bookmarked Job Listings are separate and remain until the Candidate deletes them, with closed listings marked as closed when their status can be checked.
