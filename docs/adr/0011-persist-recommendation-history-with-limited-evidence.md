# Persist recommendation history with limited evidence

JobLens stores Candidate-owned Recommendation History so people can revisit why a Job Listing was recommended, even though it does not retain the original Resume document, full Sanitized Resume, or deep Analysis Result. Each history entry may include the Job Listing identity and original link, the Candidate's selected Job Preferences, recommendation reasons, and the specific approved Resume excerpts cited as evidence. This convenience creates persistent sensitive data; entries require access control and source-specific checks before storing supplied posting text. A history entry expires after 180 days and can be deleted individually at any time; account deletion removes all remaining entries. Bookmarked Job Listings are separate and remain until the Candidate deletes them, with closed listings marked as closed when their status can be checked.
