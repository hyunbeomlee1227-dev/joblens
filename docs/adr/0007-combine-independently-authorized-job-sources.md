# Combine independently authorized Job Sources

JobLens will combine multiple Job Sources behind one discovery interface because no verified single source covers current Korean openings across all occupations. Each source and use is activated separately only after access, attribution, display, retention, and analysis rights relevant to that use are verified; a source's technical availability is not authorization. A display-only source can support discovery without implying permission to retain or analyze its content (ADR-0014). The first candidates to validate are the Gyeonggi Jobaba feed, JOB-ALIO public-sector feed, and Jooble Korea, but none is considered connected or approved yet.

## Consequences

- Each adapter returns Job Listings with source identity, source record ID, original URL, observed time, closing time when available, and only fields that source is permitted to supply. Missing fields stay unknown; they are not inferred.
- The discovery module filters closed or stale listings, deduplicates cross-posted openings, and records which source supplied each discovery or recommendation. Duplicates merge only with stable identifiers or a conservative match on employer, title, location, and original employer URL; uncertain records remain separate. A merged card retains all provenance, and no source's display, retention, or analysis permission is transferred to another source's fields. A transient adapter connection failure gets one automatic retry; after that, partial results from healthy sources remain visible with an availability notice. If all sources fail, the state is `현재 공고를 확인할 수 없음`, not an empty market. Permission failures and exhausted quotas are not retried as transient errors.
- A Job Listing with only metadata or a snippet appears as a Discovered Job Listing, not a Job Recommendation. ADR-0013 records this later distinction; reasons for discovery are restricted to supplied listing facts and Job Preferences. A deeper Analysis Job requires an authorized, analysis-permitted full Job Posting from a Job Source; the former candidate-paste fallback is retired.
- Jobaba development auto-approval does not replace its operational review; Jooble key, quota, caching, and AI-use terms need confirmation; JOB-ALIO's scope is public institutions. Unverified adapters remain disabled in a public deployment.
- The initial public discovery area is Seoul, Gyeonggi, and Incheon across multiple occupations. A useful public live discovery release requires at least one authorized source and 20 distinct, currently open Eligible Job Listings across at least two occupations, with verifiable original links; coverage is disclosed. This is the initial launch threshold, not a shutdown trigger: if the count later drops, discovery stays available with actual count and coverage shown. If the initial gate does not pass, fixtures are labeled as a demo rather than live discovery.
- The first release applies the Candidate's chosen role, region, and work arrangement as strict filters. It does not silently widen them or include overseas remote jobs; when no listings remain, it explains the empty result and lets the Candidate change their preferences.
- Listings with expired closing dates or without a verifiable open recruitment status are excluded from automatic recommendations, even if that reduces the result count.
- When duplicate sources disagree on open status or closing date, the merged listing is marked as pending verification and excluded from automatic discovery and recommendation until an authorized reliable source resolves the conflict; the service never chooses the more favorable status merely to preserve inventory.
- A Listing Report is available to authenticated Candidates; it prompts revalidation and can hide a suspect listing while its status is checked. Visitors can browse listings without signing in.
